# Press Release Resources

This repository provides a practical starting point for businesses, startups, and marketing teams preparing news announcements and planning press release distribution.

## When to Prepare a Press Release

A press release may be appropriate for newsworthy updates such as:

- Product or service launches
- Company milestones and significant business updates
- New partnerships or initiatives
- Research findings or original reports
- Events, expansions, or leadership announcements

Avoid presenting routine promotional claims as major news. Make sure statements are accurate and can be substantiated.

## Press Release Writing Checklist

Before submitting a release, confirm that it includes:

1. **Clear headline:** Summarize the news accurately.
2. **Dateline:** Include the relevant location and release date when appropriate.
3. **Opening paragraph:** Explain who, what, when, where, and why.
4. **Supporting details:** Add verified facts and useful context.
5. **Quote:** Include an authorized, accurately attributed quote when relevant.
6. **Company boilerplate:** Briefly describe the organization.
7. **Media contact:** Provide a monitored email or other appropriate contact method.
8. **Proofreading:** Check names, dates, figures, links, and spelling.

## Distribution Planning Checklist

- Confirm the announcement is newsworthy and accurate.
- Identify the intended audience and relevant distribution options.
- Review each provider's eligibility rules, reach, pricing, and reporting.
- Confirm publication expectations and whether specific outlets are guaranteed.
- Prepare approved assets and contact details.
- Keep a record of distribution and any available publication reports.

## What to Expect From Reporting

Depending on the service, reporting may include distribution confirmation, pickup information, or links to published pages. Distribution does not guarantee editorial coverage, media attention, backlinks, traffic, or search ranking improvements.
